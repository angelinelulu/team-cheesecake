# Snoot 🐾

Snoot turns your screen time into pet care. The more time you spend on apps you're trying to cut back on, the sicker your virtual pet gets — and the more time you spend on the habits you actually want to build, the happier and healthier it becomes.

## How it works

1. **Pick your pet.** Choose a dog, cat, bear, bunny, or cow to raise.
2. **Set your apps.** Tell Snoot which apps tend to eat your time (social media, endless scrolling) and which apps you want to spend more time on instead (reading, learning, fitness — whatever "better" looks like for you).
3. **Live your day.** Snoot quietly keeps track in the background — no need to check in constantly.
4. **Watch your pet react.** Too much time on the apps you're avoiding, and your pet starts to feel it. Spend time on the good stuff, and your pet thrives.
5. **Build a streak.** Keep it up day after day to earn streaks and badges.

## Why a pet?

It's easier to care about a living thing than a number on a screen. A "you spent 47 minutes on social media today" stat is easy to ignore. A pet that's visibly not doing well is harder to look away from — and a lot more motivating to fix.

## Stay connected with friends

Add friends using a simple shareable code, then visit the **Pet Park** together — a shared space where you can see how everyone's pets are doing. It's a gentle nudge of accountability: nobody wants to be the friend whose pet is clearly not okay.

## A few extra touches

- **Nearby park suggestions**, for when the best fix for too much screen time is just... going outside.
- **Gentle alerts** if you've been on an avoided app for a while, and **little celebrations** when you've put in time on the good stuff.
- **Background music and sound effects**, with a mute option if you'd rather keep things quiet.
